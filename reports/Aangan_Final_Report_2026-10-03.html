Admin Command Center (www.aanganresort.in/admin)

  • Page content, blog, offers, announcements, popups, header and footer, media library, roles and permissions, activity log.
  • Image Placements: 51 photo spots across the site, each showing where it appears, the size to upload, alt text and slug.
  • Our Events (3 empty drafts: The Creators Courtyard, Evolve, New Year Party), My Occasions, Competitors, Analytics & Search, Social Posts.

CRM and Kitchen

  • Lead lifecycle: New, Contacted, Qualified, Proposal Sent, Negotiation, Confirmed. "Closed" is a separate inactive state, not a booking.
  • Master menu: 103 items imported from your Word file (breakfast, hi-tea, lunch and dinner). The event planner picks from it, with veg and non-veg marks.
  • Kitchen: meal times chosen for the event are highlighted; the kitchen ticks items and gives a rate per meal or one package rate. Sales never sees kitchen rates, minimum price or cost.
  • "Ask for review" button on every lead sends your Google review link on WhatsApp.
