Use case description (large box, required) (paste this)
---
StatAxis is a media measurement service for Indian digital news. (1) Live audience: every 15 seconds it reads the public concurrent-viewer count of the live streams of tracked news channels and reports average and peak audience for any period. (2) Views of new videos: it reads public view counts of videos uploaded in a period and ranks channels by views gained. (3) STX Index: a composite index StatAxis calculates from these public signals. (4) Reports: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, half-yearly and yearly summaries as PDFs, images and e-mails for registered business users. Users never call the YouTube API: they see StatAxis's stored readings, each marked 'as last measured', with the line 'Data via YouTube. Processed and evaluated by StatAxis. Not TV ratings.' Independent value: publishers, advertisers and researchers get a neutral comparison of digital news audiences in India, which YouTube's own tools do not provide for other people's channels.

 QUOTA JUSTIFICATION
---
Requested: 75,000 units/day. The default is 10,000. We plan to measure more than 300 news channels (about 450 simultaneous live streams at peak) with a 15-second interval during live programmes, because live audiences move within seconds around breaking news and commercial breaks. Per day: live polling every 15 s: ceil(450/50) = 9 videos.list calls x 5,760 polls = 51,840 units. Hourly collection: 300 playlistItems.list + 6 channels.list + 150 videos.list = 456 units x 24 = 10,944 units. Total 62,784 units; with 15% headroom about 72,200, so we request 75,000. With the default we can read only about 32 channels every 75 seconds. If a smaller first grant is more appropriate, we can start with about 100 channels every 15 s and the rest every 60 s (about 42,000 units/day) and grow after the audit.
---
 DATA STORAGE AND RETENTION
---
We store time-stamped readings of public statistics (video views, likes, comment counts, live concurrent viewers, channel subscriber counts) and the metrics derived from them (averages, peaks, views gained, the STX Index) for at most 36 calendar months, after which they are deleted; this is the retention allowed by the additional policies for derived metrics and data storage, which we ask to be approved for. Other YouTube API data (video titles, thumbnails, categories, channel avatars and handles) is refreshed from the API at least every 30 calendar days, and data that has not been refreshed within 30 days is cleared automatically. An automatic retention job runs with every collection pass. Our first reading is from 10 October 2026, so no reading has reached the limit yet. Channel display names shown by StatAxis are editorial labels kept by StatAxis. We store no comment text and no personal data of viewers or commenters.
---
 DERIVED METRICS
---
Yes: averages and peaks over a period, views gained in a period, a composite index (the STX Index) from these public signals, and comparisons across channels of different publishers. We label these clearly and prominently as StatAxis's own calculation, not sourced from YouTube (on every page footer, report and file: 'Data via YouTube. Processed and evaluated by StatAxis. Not TV ratings.' and 'STX Index and other scores are generated independently by StatAxis. They are not sourced from, provided by or endorsed by YouTube.'). We agree to the additional policies for derived metrics and data storage.
---
 API METHODS AND HOW OFTEN
---
Read-only, API key only (no OAuth). playlistItems.list: 1 call per tracked channel in each hourly collection pass. channels.list: 50 channels per call, hourly. videos.list: 50 videos per call, hourly for new uploads and every 15 seconds for live streams while they are live. No search.list in the live path; no write methods; no YouTube Analytics or Reporting API; no embedded player.
---
 CURRENT QUOTA USAGE
---
About 5,300 units/day today (32 channels, live interval 75 seconds). The Quotas graph is attached.
---
 QUOTA EFFICIENCY
---
All videos.list and channels.list calls are batched at 50 ids; no search.list in the live loop; our client enforces its own budget (60 requests a minute and 9,000 a day at the default); results are cached and shared by all users; a user never triggers an API call.
---
 HOW YOU SHOW YOUTUBE DATA AND KEEP IT FRESH
---
Values are shown 'as last measured' with their time; stale readings are flagged and never presented as current. Each report shows when it was made.
---
 PRIVACY, TERMS, DELETION
---
Privacy Policy https://www.stataxis.org/privacy and Terms https://www.stataxis.org/terms state that the service uses YouTube API Services and describe retention and deletion. The footer of every public page links the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We use no Authorized Data, so nothing can be revoked in Google security settings. A user can ask for account deletion at stax9@stataxis.org; a publisher who does not want its channel measured can ask us and we remove it.

We do not sell raw API data and give users no API access. Users see reports, rankings and downloads of statistics and our calculations, each marked with its source. We do not download or republish video or audio.
